Privacy Policy for AuraSense

Last updated: July 2026

AuraSense ("we", "our", "the app") is a free air quality and weather monitoring app. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

Location data (GPS): We use your device's location to show you real-time air quality and weather data for your current city. Location is used only while the app is active and is not stored on our servers.

How We Use Your Information

Location coordinates are sent to our backend server solely to fetch air quality (AQI) and weather data for your area from public data sources (World Air Quality Index, Open-Meteo). We do not sell, rent, or share your location data with third parties for advertising or any other purpose.

Data Storage

We do not store your location history or personal identifying information on our servers.

Third-Party Services

AuraSense uses the following external services to provide data:

  • World Air Quality Index (WAQI) — air quality data
  • Open-Meteo — weather data
  • BigDataCloud — reverse geocoding (converting coordinates to city names)

Children's Privacy

AuraSense does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Continued use of the app means you accept any changes.
